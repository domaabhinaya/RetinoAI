2. Medical Vision & Clinical Diagnostic Engine

The vision engine evaluates high-resolution fundus photographs using international standards established by the American Academy of Ophthalmology (AAO) and the Early Treatment Diabetic Retinopathy Study (ETDRS):

A. International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy (ICDR) 5-Stage Disease Scale

Grade Clinical Stage Pathological Findings on Fundoscopy Recommended Action
0 No DR Normal caliber retinal vessels, intact macula, clear optic nerve margins, healthy foveal reflex. Annual routine re-screening
1 Mild NPDR Microaneurysms only (isolated focal capillary out-pouchings along temporal arcades). Repeat screening in 6–12 months
2 Moderate NPDR More than microaneurysms: scattered blot hemorrhages in 1–2 quadrants, early hard lipid exudates. Comprehensive dilated fundoscopy
3 Severe NPDR 4-2-1 Rule: Severe blot hemorrhages across all 4 quadrants, venous beading in ≥2 quadrants, or prominent IRMA in ≥1 quadrant. Urgent specialist referral (within 1–2 weeks)
4 PDR Active neovascularization of disc (NVD) or elsewhere (NVE), fibrous proliferation, preretinal/vitreous hemorrhage risk. Urgent surgical / anti-VEGF consult (within 48–72 hrs)

B. ETDRS Macular Edema (CSME) Classification

Diabetic Macular Edema (DME) is classified independently from the vascular retinopathy stage:

  • None: No retinal thickening or hard exudates in posterior pole.
  • Mild: Exudates or retinal thickening distant from the macular center.
  • Clinically Significant Macular Edema (CSME): Triggered when hard exudates or retinal thickening occur within 500 μm (1/3 disc diameter) of the central fovea, or when circinate lipid plaques envelope the foveal avascular zone.

C. Green-Channel Optical Calibration (540–570 nm)

Oxygenated and deoxygenated hemoglobin have an absorption peak at 540–570 nm (green wavelength spectrum). The RetinoAI engine applies green-channel contrast enhancement (1.48:1 contrast ratio) to demarcate microvascular leaks, punctate aneurysms, and subtle micro-hemorrhages from the darker choroidal background.
